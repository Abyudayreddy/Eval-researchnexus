| **CLM-001** | ResearchNexus provides complete and correct local provenance tracing from generated claims back to exact source text chunks. | E08 automated provenance audit evaluated 202 claims across 100 queries, achieving 100% Trace Completeness, Claim-to-Evidence Accuracy, and Citation-to-Evidence Accuracy. | `SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITATION` | Valid under automated audit protocol verification; does not establish subjective human interpretability. |
| **CLM-002** | Verified RAG significantly outperforms Naive RAG in claim factual grounding and citation precision. | E04.1 measured Naive CSR=0.0533 vs Verified CSR=0.0350 (McNemar p=0.6831). | **CLM-003** | Iterative refinement substantially improves answer factual accuracy over single-pass generation. | E07.1 evaluated 100 queries: 1 improved, 98 unchanged, 1 degraded (permutation test p=1.000). | `INVALID_DO_NOT_USE` | Empirically refuted on ds_nexus_v11. Iteration added 2.2x latency without factual quality recovery. |
| **CLM-004** | BGE Cross-Encoder reranking executes genuinely across all queries without bypass. | E02 and E09 verified 100/100 genuine BGE cross-encoder invocations with p50 latency of 531.41 ms (p95 of 4,246.05 ms). | `SUPPORTED` | Reranking operates within candidate context pools; lower-ranked gold documents amid distractors challenge generative synthesis. |
| **CLM-005** | Citation repair universally fails across all retrieval settings. | E06.1 observed 191 repair attempts with 0 successful repairs on ds_nexus_v11. | `SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITATION` | Finding is specific to evaluated context pool and conservative exact matching policy; do not claim universal failure across all domains. |
| **CLM-006** | Automated claim verifier achieves 95%+ precision against human expert annotators. | Experiment E05 is blocked due to lack of human ground-truth annotations. | `NOT_MEASURED` | Human evaluation was not conducted. No human agreement or verifier F1 metrics exist. |
| **CLM-007** | Downstream RAG generation failures predominantly follow a context-distraction and grounding-deficit propagation pattern. | E11 multi-label error taxonomy analysis revealed that despite Hit@20=1.0 at candidate level, lower ranking placement (ranks 15-17) resulted in 28 unsupported claims, 28 missing citations, 27 repair failures, and 15 iteration exhaustions. | `SUPPORTED` | Characterizes observed empirical propagation across components; not a claim of universal deterministic causality. |
