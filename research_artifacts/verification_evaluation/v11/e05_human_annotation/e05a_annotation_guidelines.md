# ResearchNexus: Human Annotation Guidelines (Protocol V1.1)

## Overview
This document provides instructions for expert human annotators evaluating claims generated by research synthesis models on the `ds_nexus_v11` benchmark.

---

## 1. Task Objective
Your task is to determine whether each **Claim** is directly and accurately supported by the **Exact Attached Evidence**, or whether it is ungrounded or contradicted.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Judge each claim **STRICTLY** against the provided evidence text. Do **NOT** judge whether the claim is plausible based on your background knowledge or general world facts. If the evidence text does not support the claim, it must be marked as `UNSUPPORTED` even if it is factually true in reality.

---

## 2. Four-Class Label Taxonomy

Annotators must assign exactly **one** of the following four labels to each claim:

| Label | Definition | Example / Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| `SUPPORTED` | The attached evidence explicitly, fully, and unambiguously entails the factual assertions in the claim. | The claim states "Model X achieves 85.2% accuracy on benchmark Y" and the chunk explicitly says "Model X achieved 85.2% accuracy on benchmark Y". |
| `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` | The attached evidence supports part of the claim or core premise, but leaves minor sub-claims, metrics, or extrapolations unsupported. | The claim states "Model X improves latency by 30% and reduces memory by 50%", but the chunk only mentions the 30% latency improvement without mentioning memory. |
| `UNSUPPORTED` | The attached evidence does not contain sufficient facts to verify the claim, no evidence is cited/attached, or the citation is completely irrelevant. | The claim makes a specific factual statement, but the cited chunk discusses an unrelated background concept or no evidence text is present. |
| `CONTRADICTED` | The attached evidence directly contradicts, refutes, or negates the factual assertion made in the claim. | The claim states "Method A outperforms Method B", but the cited evidence explicitly reports "Method B significantly outperformed Method A". |

---

## 3. Workflow & Submission

1. **Independent Blind Annotation**: Annotator 1 and Annotator 2 must complete `e05a_annotator_1_template.csv` and `e05a_annotator_2_template.csv` independently without consulting each other or examining model predictions.
2. **Annotation Columns**:
   - `annotator_label`: Must be exactly one of `SUPPORTED`, `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`.
   - `annotator_rationale`: (Optional) Free-text explanation of reasoning or specific phrases of concern.
3. **Adjudication**:
   - After both annotators complete their sheets, the Senior Adjudicator reviews instances of inter-annotator disagreement in `e05a_adjudication_template.csv` and assigns the authoritative `adjudicated_label`.
