   - Because the target IDs were selected directly from retriever candidate maps, this introduces a **POTENTIAL EVALUATION CONTAMINATION** flag.

2. **Provenance Status**:
   - The benchmark dataset `ds_nexus_v10` MUST be marked **`NOT VERIFIED`** and **`INVALID_FOR_PUBLICATION_EVALUATION`**.
   - Gold evidence cannot be considered benchmark-grade until generated by an independent process from actual document text.
