PaperQA to provide evidence-backed answers from scientific 
PDFs. Zheng et al. [10] proposed OpenResearcher, and 
Agarwal et al. [11] developed LitLLM to streamline scientific 
review authoring. While these systems demonstrate the utility 
of scientific RAG, they typically lack formal sentence-level 
verification and verifiable provenance audits. 
E. Citation Verification and Revision 
Ensuring that generated statements accurately reflect their 
cited sources is an active area of investigation. Menick et al. 
[5] emphasized supporting generative claims with verifiable 
quotes. Liu et al. [3] discovered that over 30% of generated 
claims in search engines lacked verifiable citation support. 
Gao et al. [12] introduced RARR to revise unsupported 
claims. Asai et al. [13] introduced Self-RAG, and 
subsequently developed OpenScholar [14] for scientific 
synthesis. ResearchNexus AI advances this lineage by 
implementing a modular pipeline combining deterministic 
chunk verification, semantic inference, conservative citation 
repair, and full provenance tracing. 
III. METHODOLOGY AND SYSTEM 
ARCHITECTURE 
ResearchNexus AI is structured as an auditable, multi-
stage pipeline designed to enforce evidentiary grounding at 
every step. The architecture incorporates seven discrete 
stages: corpus chunking and dual indexing, hybrid candidate 
retrieval, neural reranking, context-bounded generation, two-
stage claim verification, conservative citation repair, and 
cryptographic provenance logging. 
A. Corpus Ingestion and Dual Indexing 
Scientific publications are parsed from full-text PDF 
documents. Text is segmented into semantic passages of 300 
to 500 tokens with an overlap of 50 tokens to prevent 
information loss across chunk boundaries. The evaluation 
corpus 
comprises 
26,259 
passages 
indexed 
in 
ResearchMind_LocalKB. Each passage is indexed across two 
complementary representations: an Inverted Index for BM25 
term matching, and a persistent ChromaDB vector store 
embedded using sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2. 
B. Hybrid First-Stage Retrieval via RRF 
Given query Q, BM25 computes relevance score for 
document D: 
Score_BM25(D, Q) = ∑_{q∈Q} IDF(q) · [f(q, D) · 2.5] / [f(q, D) 
+ 1.5 · (0.25 + 0.75 · |D| / avgdl)]    (1) 
Concurrently, dense semantic retrieval computes cosine 
similarity: 
Sim_Dense(D, Q) = (v_Q · v_D) / (||v_Q|| · ||v_D||)    (2) 
Ranked lists R_BM25 and R_Dense are fused via 
Reciprocal Rank Fusion: 
RRF(d) = ∑_{m ∈ {BM25, Dense}} 1 / [60 + r_m(d)]    (3) 
where r_m(d) is the 1-based rank of passage d in system 
m. The top k=20 candidates are retained for neural reranking. 
C. Neural Cross-Encoder Reranking 
The candidate pool is reranked using BAAI/bge-reranker-
base. The model evaluates bidirectional cross-attention across 
all query and passage tokens. Passages are ordered by logit 
scores to form the prioritized evidence context C = [e₁, e₂, ..., 
e_k]. 
D. Context-Bounded Synthesis 
The sorted evidence is provided to gemini-3.5-flash-lite 
under strict prompts requiring the model to rely exclusively 
on the provided context. Factual assertions must cite numeric 
markers [1], [2]. Sampling temperature is set to τ = 0.0 to 
eliminate speculative extrapolation. 
E. Two-Stage Claim Verification and Repair 
The answer is parsed into atomic claims {c₁, c₂, ..., c_n}. 
Verification proceeds across two stages: Stage A evaluates 
deterministic token overlap (threshold θ_det = 0.80); 
unresolved claims undergo Stage B natural language 
inference. If a claim lacks support, Citation Repair searches 
remaining retrieved chunks for exact substring or high-
confidence entailment matches. 
F. Self-Feedback and Bounded Iterative Refinement 
Persisting unsupported assertions trigger structured 
critique prompts reinvoking the generator with prior draft and 
identified gaps, bounded to M=2 cycles. 
G. Provenance Ledger 
Every claim is logged to an immutable JSON ledger with 
citation indices, chunk IDs, retrieval ranks, RRF scores, 
reranking logits, and SHA256 context hashes. 
IV. EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS AND DISCUSSIONS 
All experiments were conducted on the frozen benchmark 
ds_nexus_v11 (N=100 queries: 70 DEV, 15 VAL, 15 TEST; 
26,259 corpus chunks) using gemini-3.5-flash-lite at τ = 0.0.
