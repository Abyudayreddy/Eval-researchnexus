VI. CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORK 
This paper presented ResearchNexus AI, an explainable, 
multi-stage RAG framework designed for verified scientific 
literature synthesis. By uniting BM25 and dense vector 
search through Reciprocal Rank Fusion, the system achieves 
high candidate recall (Hit@20 = 1.0000 on test queries) in 
7.83 ms median latency. BGE cross-encoder reranking 
prioritizes relevant evidence, and the automated provenance 
graph delivers 100% end-to-end auditability across all 202 
evaluated claims. 
Our empirical findings on ds_nexus_v11 provide practical 
guidance for scientific AI systems. Conservative citation 
repair successfully prevents false-positive attributions, while 
iterative refinement adds a 2.2x latency overhead without 
meaningful grounding recovery when gold context is diluted 
by 
distractors. 
Reliable 
scientific 
synthesis 
requires 
disciplined context filtering rather than blind iterative 
revision. 
Future research will explore: (1) dynamic context pruning 
based on cross-encoder logit margins to eliminate distracting 
passages; (2) domain-specific NLI verifiers tuned on 
academic corpora to differentiate cautious hedging from 
factual hallucinations; and (3) active multi-hop re-querying 
when initial evidence pools exhibit information gaps.
