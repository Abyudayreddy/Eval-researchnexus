# ResearchNexusAI — Research Integrity Questions

### 1. How did you resolve historical metric discrepancies during the audit?
- **Answer:** We conducted a four-phase forensic audit (V1 to V4), separating hardcoded documentation from raw experimental logs. Claims not supported by raw data (such as statistical reranker superiority over hybrid) were formally retracted into a Superseded Claims Register.

### 2. Why is human evaluation designated as future work?
- **Answer:** Grounding metrics were evaluated using automated NLI verifier models. Because rigorous human double-blind annotation across 687 technical papers was not completed, scientific integrity required designating human validation as planned future work.
