# ResearchNexusAI — ResearchMind Dataset Overview

> **Location:** `ResearchMind_Dataset/`  
> **Status:** Corpus Dataset Specification  

---

## 1. Corpus Scope and Domain Distribution
The evaluation and knowledge base corpus comprises **687 peer-reviewed scientific papers** across three major domains:

1. **Artificial Intelligence (`ResearchMind_Dataset/Artificial_Intelligence/`):**
   - Subdomains: Deep Learning, Machine Learning, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
   - Core Topics: Transformer architectures, neural reranking, dense passage retrieval, hallucination mitigation.

2. **Environmental Science (`ResearchMind_Dataset/Environmental_Science/`):**
   - Subdomains: Climate Change, Pollution, Sustainability, Water Resources.
   - Core Topics: Hydrological modeling, atmospheric emissions, carbon capture, renewable energy grids.

3. **Interdisciplinary Research (`ResearchMind_Dataset/Interdisciplinary/`):**
   - Subdomains: Computational Biology, Digital Twins, Green Computing, Industry 4.0, Smart Cities, Sustainable Development.
   - Core Topics: Urban flood forecasting, IoT-enabled environmental monitoring, genomics sequence alignment.

## 2. Integrity and Storage Rules
- Original PDFs remain permanently preserved under `ResearchMind_Dataset/`.
- No PDF may be deleted, renamed, or modified.
- Full text, metadata, and passage chunks are systematically extracted into `ResearchMind_LocalKB/`.
