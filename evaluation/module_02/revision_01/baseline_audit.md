# ResearchNexus AI — Module 2 Revision 01: Baseline Audit

## 1. Audit Objective
The purpose of this audit is to verify whether the baseline condition (`NAIVE_RAG`) is a genuine, unaugmented conventional RAG baseline or whether it was conflated with verification components during answer generation.

Specifically, we audited:
1. Does Naive RAG execute claim verification or citation repair *prior* to returning its generated answer?
2. Does Naive RAG receive any verification feedback or iterative refinement?
3. What is the exact sequence of operations in the experimental execution harness (`run_e04_1_corrected.py`)?

## 3. Findings & Baseline Validity Determination

1. **Answer Generation Isolation**:
   - `naive_text` is generated by a single prompt call to `LLMProviderManager.generate(question, context_text_naive)`.
   - No claim extraction, no NLI verification, no citation repair, and no refinement loops are executed before or during the synthesis of `naive_text`.
   - The answer logged as the final Naive output is identical to `naive_text`.

2. **Nature of Downstream Verification**:
   - Claim extraction and verification on `naive_text` occur **strictly offline after answer completion** solely to measure the baseline's grounding metrics.
   - The naive answer is never modified, filtered, or regenerated based on these verification checks.

3. **Baseline Integrity Verdict**:
   - **`BASELINE_VALID_RETAINED`**: The baseline implementation represents a genuine conventional context-bounded RAG generation. It does NOT execute pre-generation verification. The existing baseline raw outputs are authentic and scientifically valid to retain for reclassification.
